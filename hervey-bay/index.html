It's often how we feel afterwards that stays with us.

The Long Exhale is a new retreat. These words come from women who've joined my earlier gatherings and restorative experiences.

Why I created The Long Exhale.

Like so many women, I've had seasons where life has felt like a lot to carry.

Raising my two boys, navigating separation, grief and loss and all the responsibilities that come with life in between.

Somewhere along the way, I became so used to making sure everyone else was okay that I barely stopped to ask myself what I needed.

And I know I'm not alone in that.

One of the things I love most about the experiences we share is watching women arrive, sometimes alone, sometimes a little unsure of what to expect.

And within an hour, there's laughter, hugs, conversations and connections that simply wouldn't have happened otherwise.

It's a beautiful reminder of how much we need these moments, and each other.

Four beautiful days by the coast, away from the usual routines and responsibilities. Time to rest, breathe, enjoy beautiful food, music, laughter, meaningful conversations and the simple pleasure of having nowhere else you need to be.

A little space to reconnect with yourself, and perhaps remember parts of you that have been quietly waiting for some attention.

I'd love to share these days with you. 🤎

You're probably wondering...

Can I come on my own?

Absolutely. Many women come to my gatherings on their own. Connection happens naturally over meals and shared moments, and there's always time to enjoy your own company too.

Do I need to have done yoga or meditation before?

Not at all. Everything is gentle and you can go at your own pace.

Do I have to join in everything?

No. Everything is an invitation. If you'd rather walk, read or rest, you're welcome to.

Will I have time to myself?

Yes. There's plenty of unplanned time to read, walk, rest or do very little at all.

Can I come with a friend?

Of course. Choose the stay that suits you both.

What if I can't stay all four days?

Come as a Retreat Guest and sleep at home or nearby, or join us for one full day as a Day Guest.

Are payment plans available?

Yes. A $500 deposit holds your place, with flexible payments for the balance. Just ask.
