You don't have to know what you need.

So many women who reach out to me tell me they know they need something. They just aren't sure what.

I understand that. When you've spent years being the one who remembers, organises and notices, your own needs get very quiet.

Maybe your kids are growing up and needing you a little less. Maybe life has simply been full for a long time.

You don't have to be exhausted or going through something hard to come. You don't need a reason, a plan or anything figured out.

Maybe it's rest. Maybe it's laughter, a long lunch or a conversation that stays with you for months. You can find out when you get here.

Before you read on, take one slow breath with me.

How women feel afterwards.

The Long Exhale is a new retreat. These words come from women who've joined my earlier gatherings and restorative experiences.

Why I created The Long Exhale.

Like so many women, I've had seasons with a lot to carry. Raising my two boys, navigating separation, grief and loss, and all the things life brings along the way. I was so busy making sure everyone else was okay that I barely stopped to ask what I needed.

I know I'm not alone in that.

Rainbow Beach is my home, and it's where I get to share some beautiful experiences with women. I watch women arrive a little unsure, sometimes alone, not quite knowing what to expect. And within an hour they're hugging, laughing and talking like old friends.

The Long Exhale gives you four whole days of that feeling. Rest and quiet, with plenty of joy too. Music, long meals and new friendships.

It's a beautiful reminder of how much we need these moments, and each other.

“A little space to simply be, to listen to what you need and remember what matters to you.”

I'd love to share these days with you. 🤎

Kim x

You're probably wondering...

Can I come on my own?

Absolutely. Many women come to my gatherings on their own. Connection happens naturally over meals and shared moments, and there's always time to enjoy your own company too.

Do I need to have done yoga or meditation before?

Not at all. Everything is gentle and you can go at your own pace.

Do I have to join in everything?

No. Everything is an invitation. If you'd rather walk, read or rest, you're welcome to.

Will I have time to myself?

Yes. There's plenty of unplanned time to read, walk, rest or do very little at all.

Can I come with a friend?

Of course. Choose the stay that suits you both.

Are payment plans available?

Yes. A $500 deposit holds your place, with flexible payments for the balance. Just ask.
